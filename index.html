Honest timelines for skin and hair

  • Skin: glow and smoother texture show in 3–4 weeks; evening out tan and marks takes 8–12 weeks. Starting now is exactly on time.
  • Hair fall: hair responds slowly. Shedding usually eases 6–10 weeks after the cause is fixed, so the tests in week 1 matter more than any oil.
  • Hair growth: about 1 cm a month, so roughly 2.5 cm by the wedding. What you can change by February is shine, less breakage and less shedding.

Adjust by results, not by the calculator

Kitchen shortcuts

  • Soak at night: rajma, chana or dried peas for tomorrow, plus the almonds. Start a batch of moong sprouts twice a week.
  • Cook dal and legumes double. Half goes in the fridge and becomes tomorrow's side or the breakfast ghugni.
  • Paneer at home from toned milk is leaner than shop paneer. Keep the whey water for kneading atta or cooking dal.
  • Soya chunks: boil 5 minutes, squeeze out the water well, then cook. That removes the smell people dislike. Three or four times a week is plenty.
  • Greens four times a week (saga, palak, sajana saga) with a squeeze of lemon. The vitamin C helps your body take up the iron.
  • Eating out or at a function: fill up on dal, paneer, tandoori items and salad first; one roti or a little rice; skip the fried starters and sugary drinks.

How to progress

  • Write it down. Note the weight and reps for every set in your phone. Next session, try to beat it by one rep.
  • When every set hits the top of the rep range with clean form, add the smallest weight available next time and start again from the bottom of the range.
  • Stop each set with 1–2 good reps left (after the first two weeks). Grinding to failure adds soreness, not results.
  • Soreness in the first two weeks is normal and fades. Sharp or joint pain is not: stop that exercise and swap it.
  • Missed a day? Do it on Wednesday or Saturday. Missed a week? Just restart; don't try to make it up.
  • Machine taken? Any exercise for the same muscle works. Consistency beats the perfect plan.
Skin

Avisha's skin routine

"Brighter" realistically means even tone, less tan, fewer marks and a healthy glow. Sunscreen does more for that than every serum combined.

Morning · 3 minutes
  1. Gentle face wash (non-foaming or low-foam)
  2. Vitamin C serum 10%, from week 3
  3. Light moisturiser (gel type if skin is oily)
  4. Sunscreen SPF 50, PA++++: two finger-lengths for face and neck, every day, indoors too. Reapply if you are out past noon.
Night · 3 minutes
  1. Face wash, massaged for a full minute to lift the sunscreen
  2. Niacinamide serum 5–10%, from week 2 (tone, oil control, marks)
  3. Moisturiser
  4. Two nights a week (say Wed and Sun), from week 4: salicylic acid 2% instead of niacinamide, to keep pores clear

Rules that prevent most disasters

  • One new product at a time, two weeks apart, patch-tested behind the ear for two days first. If something stings or breaks you out, you will know which one.
  • Nothing new after 10 January. The last four weeks are for skin that is calm and predictable.
  • Body: moisturise right after bathing, scrub or ubtan (besan, curd, a pinch of haldi) once a week, sunscreen on arms and neck when outdoors, lip balm at night.
  • Don't pick. A picked pimple leaves a dark mark that lasts months. Use a pimple patch.
  • Change pillowcases twice a week and keep hair oil off your face at night.
Avoid: "fairness" or "instant glow" creams sold over the counter or mixed by a parlour (many contain steroids that thin the skin and cause rebound acne), lemon juice or baking soda on the face, and glutathione drips. If you are planning a pregnancy soon after the wedding, also avoid retinol and retinoids.

Shubham's skin routine

Three steps, twice a day
  1. Face wash morning and night, and after the gym. If the skin is oily, use one with salicylic acid.
  2. Moisturiser, a light gel is enough.
  3. Sunscreen SPF 50 in the morning. It is the difference between a tanned, tired face and a clear one in the photos.

Also: shower soon after training to avoid back and chest acne, wash gym clothes after each use, and use a clean towel on the face. Sharing Avisha's niacinamide serum at night is fine.

Hair routine

Every week
  • Oil once or twice a week: coconut oil on the lengths and a 5-minute fingertip scalp massage, 1–2 hours before washing. Overnight is not needed.
  • Wash 2–3 times a week with a mild shampoo, scalp only. Wash after sweaty gym days; a sweaty scalp left for days sheds more.
  • Conditioner every wash, mid-length to ends, never on the scalp.
  • Once a week: curd or aloe mask on the lengths for 20 minutes, for shine.
Handle with care
  • Wide-tooth comb, starting from the ends. Never brush wet hair hard.
  • Squeeze dry with a cotton T-shirt or microfibre towel; no rubbing.
  • Loose braid or low bun for the gym and for sleep. Tight ponytails pull hair out at the hairline.
  • Satin pillowcase or scarf at night.
  • Keep heat styling for trial days and the functions, always with a heat protectant.
  • No colouring, smoothening or keratin treatment after early January. If you want one, do it in December.

Shubham: shampoo every 1–2 days after the gym, conditioner twice a week, and the same blood tests. A haircut about 10 days before, with a tidy-up 2 days before.

Rosemary oil and onion juice have thin evidence. They won't hurt if patch-tested, but they are not a substitute for fixing iron, B12 and protein.

  • Make it automatic: 2 glasses on waking, 1 before each meal, a full bottle at the gym, 1 mid-morning and 1 mid-afternoon. That is already most of the target.

  • Water keeps skin plump and helps training and digestion, but it won't clear acne by itself. More than these amounts adds nothing.

Sleep & stress

  • 7–8 hours, same bedtime. Muscle is built and skin is repaired during sleep. Short sleep also raises hunger and sugar cravings the next day.
  • Phones away 30 minutes before bed. Wedding planning chats can wait for morning.
  • A 10-minute walk after dinner helps digestion and blood sugar, and is a good time to talk about anything except the guest list.
  • Alcohol: ideally none until the wedding. It hurts sleep, skin and fat loss all at once.
  • One planned free meal a week (Sunday lunch) keeps the rest of the week easy to follow.
Finish line

Wedding week · 1–5 Feb

  • Training: two light full-body sessions (Monday and Wednesday at the latest), half your usual sets. Nothing that leaves you sore or risks a strain. Walk daily.
  • Food: the same home food. No crash diet, no skipping meals, no new dishes or supplements. Go easy on very salty and fried food from Wednesday to avoid puffiness.
  • Water: normal amounts. Don't cut water to "look lean"; it only makes skin dull and brings headaches.
  • Skin and hair: the routine you have been doing, nothing new. Carry your own products to the venue.
  • At the functions: eat a proper protein meal before you leave home so you aren't running on sweets and snacks.
  • Sleep is the best thing you can do for your face that week.
After the wedding: keep the four gym days. The habit you have built by then is the real result; the wedding is only the first checkpoint.
